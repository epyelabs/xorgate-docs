## The one-paragraph version

An **organization** owns **workspaces**, and a workspace owns **devices**. Each
device is an instance of a **device model**, whose `io_capabilities` declare what
that hardware kit can do. A running device publishes **telemetry** as
`group.field` metrics roughly once a second, and may also record video and
telemetry locally and upload it as **segments**, which the platform indexes into
**sessions** for replay.

Everything a device does splits cleanly in two. The **live** planes (MQTT
telemetry and WebRTC video) are reached directly rather than through the REST
API and offer no history. The **recorded** plane goes through object storage and
the API, and offers no immediacy. Most of the confusion people have with this
platform comes from expecting one to behave like the other.

The tenancy model itself is covered in
[Getting started](/getting-started/tenancy), because you need it before you
can make a single request. What happens when a device changes hands — which
workspace owns it, what travels with it, and what is deliberately left behind —
is [Device ownership and transfer](/platform-concepts/device-ownership).
