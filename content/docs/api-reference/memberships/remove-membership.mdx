          OWN membership (leave); addressing someone else's with a lower role is
          a 403. Removing the last remaining owner is refused with a 403 either
          way, so an organization can never be left ownerless — hand ownership
          over first, or delete the organization.

          membership, their live-plane access is revoked too (see `DELETE
          /live/access`), and any live subscription they hold dies on its next
          reconnect. Members who still belong to another organization keep live
          access.
