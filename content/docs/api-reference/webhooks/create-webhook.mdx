          Creates an endpoint and its signing secret, returned exactly once as
          `secret` (`whsec_` + base64 of 32 random bytes, the Standard Webhooks
          format). Deliveries carry `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp` and
          `webhook-signature` headers; verify them with any Standard Webhooks
          library.


          `name` is the handle a workflow template's `output.event` node
          references in its `webhook` field; it is unique within the
          organization. `url` must be `https://`, carry no credentials, and not
          be `localhost` or a literal private, loopback or link-local address.
          Redirects are never followed.


          An endpoint receives nothing until something routes to it: a device
          route (`PUT /devices/{id}/webhooks`) or a workflow node naming it.
          `POST /webhooks/{id}/test` sends a `ping`.
