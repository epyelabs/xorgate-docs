      - content: >-
          One response feeds a whole replay: per-session timelines (effective
          durations and gaps, computed server-side so every client renders the
          same timeline) plus a presigned GET URL per segment. Address it by
          `sessionId` (single session) OR by `from`/`to` (every session
          overlapping the range, segments clipped to it, optional `streamKey`
          filter), never both. Caps: 600 segments in total, and in range mode 10
          sessions and 24 hours. Exceeding a cap is a 400 telling you to narrow
          the request. Refetch before `urlExpiresAt` (epoch ms), which is the
          real URL TTL minus a 5-minute safety margin.


          **Telemetry rides along.** `replay.telemetry` lists the
          recorded-telemetry sessions that overlap the replay window (in session
          mode, the merged video timeline; in range mode, `from`..`to`), each
          with a presigned `overview.v1` artifact and its raw segments clipped
          to the window, plus the session's `insights`. Nothing in it touches a
          database or a Lambda on fetch: the browser GETs the objects straight
          from storage, and the URLs share `urlExpiresAt` with the video ones.
          An `open` session has `overview: null`; build it from its `segments`
          and poll the manifest about every 60 s until it closes. Telemetry has
          its own cap of 1,500 segments, honoured by truncation
          (`telemetry.truncated: true`), not by a 400. Pass `telemetry=0` to
          omit the block; a device that recorded nothing in the window returns
          `{ "sessions": [] }`.
