      - content: >-
          The counterpart to `POST /iot/attach-policy`: detaches the browser
          policy from the Cognito identity behind your bearer token, after which
          the broker refuses that identity's next CONNECT and any live
          subscription dies on its next reconnect. Idempotent — revoking access
          you do not have returns the same 200.


          Self-service only. Revoking someone else's live access is a
          consequence of removing their membership: `DELETE /memberships/{id}`
          detaches automatically when the removal takes away their LAST
          organization membership. It deliberately does not fire while the user
          still belongs to another organization, because the browser policy is
          one shared org-blind policy and they remain entitled to it.


          Unlike the grant, this needs only a valid token and no organization
          membership: someone who has just lost their last membership must still
          be able to clean up after themselves.
