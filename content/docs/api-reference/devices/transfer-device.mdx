          Changes which workspace — and therefore which organization — owns a
          device.


          **What moves.** Everything. The device record, its IoT identity and
          certificates, its video channels, ALL of its historical telemetry, and
          ALL of its recorded footage. Telemetry and media are stored against
          the device, not against a tenant, so they travel with it and nothing
          is purged. For a transfer between organizations that is a
          data-disclosure event; call the preview first and show the operator
          what it says.


          **What does not move.** Workflow attachments pointing at the source
          organization's templates are DETACHED (the destination cannot see
          those templates), and the names are returned in
          `transfer.detachedWorkflowAttachments`. Workflow runs already
          executing are left to finish. Session tokens scoped to the source
          workspace stop seeing the device and are not revoked. Completed run
          history, dataset items and the registration record stay where they are
          — they are history of the device under its former owner.


          **The device does not have to be online.** Nothing on the device
          changes: certificates and identity are untouched and the tenancy is
          never stored on the box. An offline device has its new tenancy staged
          in the cloud and adopts it the moment it next connects — no reboot, no
          re-provisioning, no site visit. That is a normal success, reported
          through `transfer.warnings`, not an error.


          **Completion is two-step, and the response says which step it is at.**
          The ownership change is complete and authoritative the moment this
          returns 200. Whether the DEVICE has caught up is a separate question,
          answered by `transfer.adoption`: `pending` is the normal immediate
          answer, `confirmed` appears once the device reports publishing under
          the new tenancy, and `unknown` means it has never reported a telemetry
          scope (every agent older than v0.0.6). Poll `GET /devices/{id}` to
          watch it settle.


          **Cross-organization.** Pass `organizationId` to move the device into
          another organization you personally administer (`owner` or `admin` in
          BOTH). Without that shared membership — a genuine handover to a
          customer — use a transfer offer instead: `POST
          /devices/{id}/transfer-offers`. API keys cannot use the
          shared-membership path at all, because a key is bound to one
          organization by its own record.


          **Operational precondition.** A device whose agent predates `v0.0.6`
          has no fallback to the unscoped telemetry topic, so a transfer that it
          cannot immediately act on can leave it unable to publish until its
          agent process is restarted. Update the fleet before transferring
          fielded devices.
