      - content: >-
          Deletes the history a device has accumulated while keeping the device
          itself: its row, identity and certificate, live video channels,
          settings, webhook routes and the latest-value cache all stay, and the
          agent keeps running. What goes is chosen per `scope`:


          - `workflows`: every workflow attached to the device is detached, and
          every production run those attachments produced (and the runs of
          workflows detached earlier) is deleted with its steps, events and S3
          artifacts. Runs still executing are stopped. The organization's
          workflow templates and their simulation runs are untouched.

          - `telemetry`: recorded telemetry sessions, their segments, overview
          artifacts, ingest log and every stored reading.

          - `media`: recorded video sessions, their segments and thumbnails.


          Everything older than the moment of the request (`cutoff`) is removed;
          what the device uploads afterwards stays. A recording in progress is
          cut at that point. The work runs asynchronously: this returns `202`
          with the purge row, and `GET /devices/{id}/purges/{purgeId}` reports
          progress until `status` is `succeeded` or `failed`. One purge per
          device at a time. There is no undo.


          Organization-wide: a workspace-scoped session token is refused
          (`WORKSPACE_SCOPED`).
